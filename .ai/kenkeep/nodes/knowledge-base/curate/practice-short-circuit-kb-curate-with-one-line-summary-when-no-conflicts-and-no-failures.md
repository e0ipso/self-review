When no done/unprocessed sessions exist, report that there are no pending logs. Otherwise persist surviving actions, rebuild indices and finish the trigger-driven rebalance phase. If there are no conflicts, report written-node placements, structural results and the curation summary, then stop without entering conflict resolution. Surface persistence failures separately.

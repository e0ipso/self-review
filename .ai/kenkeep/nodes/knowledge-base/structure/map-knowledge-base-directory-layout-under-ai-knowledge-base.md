Knowledge leaves live under `.ai/kenkeep/nodes/<topic>/<id>.md`, with nested topical folders and generated `index.md` nodes for navigation. Identity is the `kk_id`, independent of placement. Leaves are `practice` or `map`; kinds do not determine directories. `_sessions/` holds captured transcripts, `_logs/` holds run logs, and `conflicts/` holds pending contradictions. `ENTRY.md`, `GRAPH.md` and the branch indices are regenerated by `npx kenkeep index rebuild`.
